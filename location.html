The Advantage of Staying in Mansarover

Mansarovar is renowned as one of Asia's largest and most well-planned urban residential areas. Staying at Hotel Sarvika gives you the distinct advantage of avoiding old walled city congestion while staying merely 5 minutes away from the Jaipur Metro terminal.

Whether arriving via train at Jaipur Junction, flight into Jaipur International Airport (JAI), or express highway coach, our location on Mangyawas Road is instantly searchable by Uber, Ola, Rapido, and local pre-paid autos.

Jaipur Transit Compass

Distances from Hotel Sarvika

Actual driving estimates and distances to Jaipur's major transit points and iconic palaces.

• 1.8 KM • ~5 Mins Drive

Mansarover Metro Station

The terminal of Jaipur's Pink Line metro. Provides smooth, air-conditioned rapid connectivity across the city straight to Railway Station, Sindhi Camp bus stand, and Chandpole Pink City market.

• 9.5 KM • ~20 Mins Drive

Jaipur Junction Railway Station

Jaipur's primary railway junction with daily Vande Bharat Express, Shatabdi, Rajdhani, and Superfast express connections to Delhi, Mumbai, Udaipur, Agra, and Ahmedabad.

• 11 KM • ~25 Mins Drive

Jaipur International Airport (JAI)

Located in Sanganer, easily accessible via wide arterial roads without heavy bottlenecks. Flights to all Indian metro cities and international gulf hubs.

• 9 KM • ~18 Mins Drive

World Trade Park (WTP)

South Jaipur's landmark shopping and architectural sensation on JLN Marg, housing premium international fashion brands, movie multiplexes, and food courts.

• 12 KM • ~28 Mins Drive

Hawa Mahal & Bapu Bazaar

The crown jewel 953-window 'Palace of Winds' and the bustling heritage bazaar for authentic Jaipuri textiles, quilts (Jaipuri Razai), blue pottery, and lac bangles.

• 13 KM • ~30 Mins Drive

City Palace & Jantar Mantar

The royal residence of the Maharaja of Jaipur featuring courtyards, armory museum, and the adjacent UNESCO World Heritage astronomical observatory.

• 18 KM • ~40 Mins Drive

Amber Fort (Amer)

Jaipur's grand hilltop 16th-century fortress, renowned for the Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace), Maota Lake reflections, and nighttime light & sound shows.

• 14 KM • ~32 Mins Drive

Nahargarh Fort

Perched on the edge of the Aravalli hills, offering breathtaking panoramic sunset vistas overlooking the illuminated Pink City skyline.

• 1.2 KM • ~3 Mins Drive

Local Mangyawas Markets

ATM machines, 24/7 pharmacies, medical centers, grocery supermarkets, and tea stalls are within comfortable walking distance along Mangyawas Road.
