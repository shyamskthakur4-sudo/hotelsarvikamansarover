Strategic Location

Mansarover & Jaipur Destination Guide

Situated in Monika Vihar, Mangyawas Road, Hotel Sarvika offers straightforward connectivity to Jaipur's major transit terminals and historical marvels.

• 1.8 KM • ~5 Mins Drive

Mansarover Metro Station

Jaipur's Pink Line metro terminal providing rapid transit across the city directly to Sindhi Camp, Railway Station, and Chandpole Pink City.

• 9.5 KM • ~20 Mins Drive

Jaipur Junction Railway Station

Primary railway terminal with Vande Bharat, Shatabdi, and Rajdhani connectivity to Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, and Agra.

• 11 KM • ~25 Mins Drive

Jaipur International Airport (JAI)

Smooth connectivity via Tonk Road / JLN Marg for hassle-free domestic and international flight departures.

• 9 KM • ~18 Mins Drive

World Trade Park (WTP)

Jaipur's landmark modern commercial hub, premium retail shopping destination, luxury cineplexes, and international food court.

• 12 KM • ~28 Mins Drive

Hawa Mahal & Bapu Bazaar

The world-famous 'Palace of Winds' and traditional heritage markets for Jaipuri quilts, handicrafts, blue pottery, and jewelry.

• 13 KM • ~30 Mins Drive

City Palace & Jantar Mantar

The majestic royal seat of the Maharaja of Jaipur, featuring courtyards, museums, and the UNESCO World Heritage astronomical observatory.

Mandatory ID Verification

All adult guests (18+) must present valid government-issued original photo ID at check-in (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License; PAN cards not accepted).

Occupancy & Visitors

Maximum 2 registered adult guests per room. In accordance with hotel security standards, outside unregistered visitors are strictly not permitted inside guest rooms.
